One world, endless creatures.

We build Zeramon Adventures around four ideas: catch them, fight with them live, discover new regions, and share your collection.

Capture

Track down wild Zeramons across every region and add them to your team.

Battle

Fight in real time against players and trainers. Every move happens live, so reflexes and timing decide the winner.

Explore

Cross forests, caves and cities, and uncover secrets hidden in every corner of the map.

Trade

Swap Zeramons with friends, complete your collection and chase the rarest finds.
